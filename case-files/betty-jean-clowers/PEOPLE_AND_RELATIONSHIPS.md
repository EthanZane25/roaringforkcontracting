# People and Relationships — Betty Jean Clowers Estate

> Working investigative index. A name or relationship appearing here is not an accusation of wrongdoing. Items are labeled as documented, reported, or requiring verification.

## Betty Jean Clowers
- Decedent.
- Longstanding connection to 1661 Lawton Avenue, Macon, Georgia.
- 2008 estate plan and August 4, 2025 will should be compared provision-by-provision.
- 2025 will states South Carolina domicile; this requires independent verification against residence, medical, tax, voter, driver's-license, utility, and facility records.

## Kayveon / Keyvean Clowers
- Identified in the probate materials as Betty's biological son / heir at law.

- This must be compared against the actual Sheriff's Return and any affidavit/testimony from the recipient and deputy.

## Frances Brooks Thornton
### Documented from probate materials
- Named in the August 4, 2025 will as Betty's "daughter and caretaker."
- Receives Betty's interest in the Lawton Avenue property under the 2025 will, subject to the exact language of the instrument.
- Named as successor personal representative after Dave Dennis III.
- Later became petitioner/personal representative after Dave Dennis III declined.
- Probate petition states Frances is **not a biological or adopted daughter** and describes her as Betty's **goddaughter**.

### Reported and requiring independent proof
- Family reports Frances was a paid caregiver.
- Family suspects Frances may have been involved in life-insurance cash-outs. No conclusion should be made until insurer transaction records identify the policy owner, signer, authority used, payee, payment method, and destination account.
- Family believes Frances may at some point have obtained an ownership interest in 1661 Lawton Avenue. The actual deed chain must establish whether, when, and how this occurred.

### Questions to answer
1. When did Frances first meet Betty?
2. When did she first become a caregiver, and who paid her?
3. Was she employed through an agency or directly?
4. When did Betty begin residing with or near Frances in South Carolina, if she did?
5. Who arranged the August 4, 2025 will appointment?
6. Who transported Betty to the signing?
7. Who gave the drafting lawyer the family/beneficiary information?
8. Who paid the lawyer?
9. Was Frances present for any attorney-client discussion or signing?
10. Did Frances possess the original will afterward?
11. Did Frances hold a POA, health-care POA, bank authority, joint account, beneficiary designation, deed interest, or insurance authority?
12. Who authorized Betty's cremation, and what relationship/authority was represented on the authorization?

## Relationship contradiction requiring explanation
The key documentary discrepancy is not simply that Frances was called "daughter." The investigation should determine **why** the 2025 will used "daughter and caretaker" while the later probate petition expressly clarified that she was not Betty's biological or adopted daughter and instead was a goddaughter. Possible innocent explanations must be considered alongside possible drafting/procurement issues. The drafting attorney's file is therefore a priority record.
