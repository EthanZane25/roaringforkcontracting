# Investigation Timeline — Betty Jean Clowers

This chronology separates documented events from reported/unverified events.

## Documented events

### April 1, 2008
Betty Jean Clowers executed an earlier will in Georgia. Based on the copy supplied by family, that will:

- appointed Andre Washington and Dave Dennis III as executors, with Tiffany Clowers Hall as successor;
- gave Donald Wright a life estate in 1661 Lawton Avenue;
- after termination of the life estate, directed disposition for Keyvean Sheheed Clowers, Tiffany Clowers Hall, Andre Washington, Dave Dennis III, Starla Wright, and Bertha Anderson;
- included burial/funeral language;
- addressed life-insurance policies and other assets.

The original 2008 will images should be preserved separately and compared line-by-line with the 2025 instrument.

### 2009
Public property-history sources reportedly show a $0/non-arm's-length quitclaim or title event involving 1661 Lawton Avenue. The actual recorded deed has not yet been obtained, so the parties and purpose remain unverified.

### August 4, 2025
A later will was executed. It describes Frances Brooks Thornton as Betty's "daughter and caretaker" and gives Frances Betty's interest in 1661 Lawton Avenue if Frances survives Betty, followed by a direction that Frances sell the property. Dave Dennis III is nominated as personal representative, with Frances as successor.

The will states Betty was a resident/domiciliary of Aiken County, South Carolina.

### October 24, 2025
Probate docket lists Betty Jean Clowers as deceased on this date.

### February 6, 2026
Dave Dennis III signed a renunciation/declination to serve as personal representative and consented to Frances Brooks Thornton serving as successor personal representative.

### March 5, 2026
Frances filed a Petition to Probate Will in Solemn Form in Bibb County Probate Court.

The petition states that Frances is **not Betty's biological or adopted daughter** and describes her instead as Betty's **goddaughter**. It identifies Kayveon Clowers as Betty's son/biological child and heir.

### March 16, 2026
Probate docket records:

- Order for Service of Notice
- Notice
- Certificate of Service
- Letter
- Return of Sheriff

The court/service materials directed personal service on Kayveon/Keyvean at the Macon address listed in the probate filing.

### April 8, 2026
PruittHealth-Augusta Hills, LLC filed a creditor claim in the estate.

### April 22, 2026
Probate court admitted the will and issued Letters Testamentary. The order states that heirs at law were served or acknowledged service.

## Reported events requiring proof

### Before death — date unknown
Family reports that Frances served as a **paid caregiver**, not a biological/adopted daughter. Obtain payroll, caregiver-agency, tax, facility, or bank records.

### Before death — date unknown
Family reports that one or more life-insurance policies were cashed out. Obtain insurer records showing policy owner, beneficiary history, surrender date, surrender request, signer, authority relied on, payment amount, and destination of proceeds.

### Around final illness — date unknown
Family reports Betty was treated at Piedmont Hospital in Macon. Complete records should be obtained, especially for the months surrounding August 4, 2025.

### After death — date unknown
Family reports Betty was cremated in South Carolina. Obtain cremation authorization, disposition permit, funeral-home contract, identity of authorizing agent, and claimed legal authority.

### March/April 2026 — service dispute
Family reports the sheriff/deputy did **not** personally serve Kayveon/Keyvean and instead left papers with his ex-girlfriend, who said she would give them to him. This must be compared against the actual Sheriff's Return and any deputy notes/body-camera/CAD/service logs if available.

## Key timing observation

The August 4, 2025 will was executed approximately 81 days before the October 24, 2025 death date shown in the probate record. That timing does not invalidate a will by itself, but it makes contemporaneous medical, caregiver, drafting-attorney, witness, and financial records especially important.
