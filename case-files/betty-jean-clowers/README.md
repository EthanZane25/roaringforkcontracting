# Betty Jean Clowers Estate Investigation

> Public working case file. This repository is public. This file distinguishes verified documents from reported facts and investigative leads. It does **not** accuse any person of a crime.

## Case

- Decedent: Betty Jean Clowers
- Bibb County Probate Court case: 26PV49392
- Georgia property discussed: 1661 Lawton Avenue, Macon, Georgia
- Date of death shown in probate docket: October 24, 2025
- 2025 will date: August 4, 2025
- Solemn-form probate petition filed: March 5, 2026
- Order admitting will: April 22, 2026

## Central documented discrepancies

1. The August 4, 2025 will describes **Frances Brooks Thornton** as Betty's "daughter and caretaker."
2. Frances's later verified probate petition states that she is **not a biological or adopted daughter** and instead describes her as Betty's **goddaughter**.
3. The probate docket nevertheless labels Frances as "DAUGHTER" while identifying **Kayveon Clowers (referred to in the will as Keyvean Sheheed Clowers)** as Betty's son and heir at law.
4. The 2025 will materially changes the disposition of 1661 Lawton Avenue from the 2008 will shown by the family. Under the 2025 will, Frances receives Betty's interest in the property if she survives Betty and is directed to sell the property.
5. Dave Dennis III was nominated as personal representative in the 2025 will, later declined, and Frances became successor personal representative/executor.
6. The probate docket contains an Order for Service of Notice, Notice, Certificate of Service, Letter, and Return of Sheriff before the April 22, 2026 order admitting the will.

## Reported facts requiring independent verification

The following are allegations or reported facts and should not be stated as established until supported by records:

- Frances was a paid caregiver rather than a daughter.
- Kayveon/Keyvean was not personally served; the papers were reportedly left with an ex-girlfriend who said she would give them to him.
- Life-insurance policies were allegedly cashed out before Betty's death.
- Betty was reportedly cremated in South Carolina despite an earlier 2008 will expressing a preference for burial.
- Frances may at some point have been added as co-owner of 1661 Lawton Avenue.
- There may have been a 2009 quitclaim/title event involving the property; the actual deed must be obtained before attributing it to Frances.

## Main investigative theories to examine

- Testamentary capacity on August 4, 2025.
- Undue influence or procurement of the 2025 will by a person in a confidential/caregiver relationship.
- Who arranged, paid for, drafted, witnessed, and notarized the 2025 will.
- Whether the description of Frances as "daughter" originated with Betty, Frances, the drafter, or another person.
- Whether any real-estate interest was transferred to Frances during Betty's lifetime.
- Whether life-insurance policies were surrendered, by whom, under what authority, and where proceeds went.
- Whether the biological son received legally sufficient service before solemn-form probate.
- Who authorized cremation in South Carolina and what legal authority was asserted.
- Betty's actual domicile and residence history between Georgia and South Carolina.
