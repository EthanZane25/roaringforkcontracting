## Preservation

Before records are lost, send preservation requests to the hospital, facility, funeral home/crematory, sheriff, drafting attorney, banks, insurers, and any caregiver agency. A preservation request is not the same thing as a subpoena and does not guarantee disclosure, but it can put the custodian on notice not to destroy potentially relevant material.
