How to Play

Winning

Both players start at $0. The first player to reach $1,000,000 wins immediately.

Turns

The current player chooses one game. Finish its result, then press Complete Turn.

Bankruptcy

Each player gets one rescue wheel spin after their first bankruptcy. A second bankruptcy loses the game.

Blackjack

Build a wager with chips, then deal from one shuffled 52-card deck.

Shut the Box

Each player rolls and flips down wooden pegs that add to the dice total. Lowest open-peg score wins $150,000.

Guess the Number

Choose an equal wager. The closer player takes the wager from the other player; a tie moves no money.
